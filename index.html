Now: Eesa AI

Eesa lets a business run its operations by asking. It connects to the systems a company already uses, builds the screens its team needs, and does the work, on the web, on iPhone and Android, and in WhatsApp. It is live in production.

1,400+

commits across nine repositories since June 2026, on every layer of the product. The code is private; I am happy to walk through any of it.

Visit eesa.ai
  • Mobile appPrimary contributor to the iOS and Android app, built with Flutter: chat, the Flow Board, and sharing a file to Eesa from any other app.
  • Add-onsPrimary contributor to five add-ons the agent uses as tools: Attendance, Drive, Invoices, Calls and QuickBooks.
  • BackendWorkflows that run on a schedule and wait for a person's answer, role-based access, and approval before the agent writes to an outside system. Python.
  • Web appThe same screens and flows in the browser, in Next.js and TypeScript.
